# Mechanical Design

This folder contains the mechanical design of our MASAAR VISION autonomous car.

We wanted our car to be compact, strong, and easy to repair or modify during testing.

Our current design uses:
- Four wheels
- Front-wheel steering
- One steering servo
- Rear-wheel drive
- One DC geared motor
- A mechanically connected rear axle
- A replaceable motor adapter
- Custom 3D-printed chassis and mechanical parts

## Our Design Process

We started by thinking about the size of the WRO track and the space needed for the electronics and sensors.

We designed the chassis to keep the main components low and near the center of the car. We also wanted enough space around the wheels for steering without touching the chassis.

The motor mount was designed as a separate part. This allows us to test different motors without printing a completely new chassis.

The design is still being physically tested. We will add our CAD files, printed parts, measurements, photos, problems, and improvements to this folder as we continue building the car.
